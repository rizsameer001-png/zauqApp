# Zauq Application — Complete Requirements Document

# 1. Project Overview

## Product Vision

Zauq is a premium English,Hindi, Urdu poetry, literature, ghazal, shayari, audiobook, ebook, and cultural learning platform inspired by modern literary ecosystems such as rekhta.org, but with a more immersive AI-powered social experience. Vey Beutifull Homepage design with navbar mega menu and footer. by default english

* Urdu poetry archive

* Poet communities
* AI literary tools
* Audio/video streaming
* Mushaira ecosystem
* Ebook library
* Urdu learning system
* Social engagement
* Creator monetization
* AI translation/transliteration
* Advanced Urdu search
